# Code of Conduct

Can I Get In? is a public-benefit project. Contributors are expected to act with respect, patience, and care.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing experiences with disability, access, mobility, and public spaces.
- Focus critique on ideas and implementation.
- Protect the privacy and safety of people represented in photos or reports.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private personal information.
- Uploading invasive, unsafe, or unlawful photos.
- Shaming individual businesses or people.

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or restrict participation when conduct harms the project or community.
